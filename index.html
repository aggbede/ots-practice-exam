Test-taking reminders from the study guide

  • RTFQ & RTFA: read the full question and every answer before choosing.
  • Full Range Leadership: the answer that blends styles is usually right. Never pick laissez-faire.
  • Military planning: if you see “analyze,” think wargaming.
  • Conflict: decide how important the task is and how important the people are, then choose Evade, Comply, Insist, Settle, or Cooperate.
